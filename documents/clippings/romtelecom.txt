Public Telephony Systems
One of the developing projects is the manufacture, supply and maintenance of the public cardphones network (hardware, software, smart cards, network management) on the basis of a contract awarded by ROMTELECOM. So far, over 35,000 INTRACOM designed cardphones have been produced and installed all over the country. Currently, INTRAROM is implementing a new three-year contract of at least 8,000 cardphones/year and is involved in technical support and development of the National Cardphone Management System.

Starting 2000, all INTRAROM cardphones installed in ROMTELECOM network are functioning on the basis of EUROCHIP 2 technology in order to provide the latest security standards to the public telephony network in Romania.

Related to this project, INTRAROM together with GEMPLUS, leader in smart cards industry, delivers annually more than 6,000,000 smart cards to ROMTELECOM. INTRAROM has also developed and delivered the Card Transaction Collecting and Management System.
