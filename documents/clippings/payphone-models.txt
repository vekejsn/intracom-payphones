    <td valign="top" width="250" colspan="2"><font face="Arial, Helvetica"><p class="Standard">The MUST (Multiple Service Terminal) cardphone is the advanced solution of INTRACOM to public telephony. By utilizing the ISDN capabilities, the MUST cardphone allows the operator to provide value added services, while it retains all features of INT cardphone series. The MUST cardphone is a new member of INTRACOM cardphone system line. Supervised by the Cardphone Supervision and Maintenance System (CSMS), MUST cardphone is the ideal path to ISDN technology. For existing cardphone networks, it is a low cost solution for new ISDN installations or for direct replacement of ordinary
      cardphones.<br>
