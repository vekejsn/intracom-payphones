Thuraya Develops More Payphone Solutions With Intracom
3 May 2003

Abu Dhabi, UAE--Thuraya announced a joining venture with Intracom, whereby Intracom will supply Thuraya with outdoor payphones. According to the agreement, Intracom will supply Thuraya with 500 outdoor payphones, 10,000 chip cards, and Payphone Management System. This commercial alliance signifies Thuraya consistent drive toward providing total mobile solutions to its customers, by working with well-known telecom service providers.

The agreement was signed last week between Thuraya and Intarcom. Present from Thuraya was Thuraya Chief Executive, Mr. Yousuf Al Sayed, while Mr. Bocar A. BA, Deputy General Manager of Intracom Middle East FZE was representing his company.

On the occasion, Thuraya's Chief Executive, Mr. Yousuf Al-Sayed stated "We are very pleased to sign this agreement with Intracom, which is considered one of the largest providers of telecom services and system devices in Greece. The payphone is one of the solutions Thuraya developed for rural telecommunications in order to connect people in different part of the globe."

Mr. Al-Sayed added, "The payphone solution is aimed at serving the remote areas most in demand of telecommunication services. We will connect these areas with the rest of the world, contributing towards the development of rural areas. Users will benefit from cost effective satellite services throughout Thuraya's coverage area. We have successfully launched pilot payphone units and tested them in various climatic conditions in many countries."

On his part, Mr. Bocar A. BA, Deputy General Manager of Intracom Middle East FZE said, "We are proud to sign this remarkable partnership agreement with Thuraya. We have worked hard with Thuraya to develop the outdoor payphone, and we are happy to contribute in developing the rural areas within Thuraya's coverage area. We are particularly proud to say that what we have developed will serve a wide range of customers in the Satellite footprint."

The above agreement will give millions of people in rural areas a chance to make phone calls at affordable price. In addition, Thuraya customers will benefit from other Thuraya services within the Thuraya satellite footprint, which covers Europe, Middle East, North and Middle Africa, South East and Central Asia. Thuraya, by working with Intracom, will continue to provide the best telecom solutions and bring about innovative technologies to its customers.
